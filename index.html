1. Chicken Karahi

★★★★★ (4.7)  •  ⏱ 30 min

Spicy chicken cooked in a wok with tomatoes and green chillies.

Ingredients
  • Chicken 1kg, Tomatoes 4, Ginger-Garlic paste 2 tbsp, Spices.
Method
  1. Fry chicken, add ginger-garlic paste.
  2. Add sliced tomatoes and spices, bhuno till oil separates.
Popular

2. Chicken Biryani

★★★★★ (4.8)  •  ⏱ 45 min

Aromatic basmati rice cooked layered with flavorful chicken curry.

Ingredients
  • Basmati Rice 750g, Chicken 1kg, Yogurt 1 cup, Biryani masala.
Method
  1. Prepare spicy chicken gravy.
  2. Boil rice and layer with chicken gravy; simmer (dam) for 15 mins.
New

3. Shahi Kheer

★★★★★ (4.9)  •  ⏱ 25 min

Traditional rice pudding cooked slow with milk, cardamom, and nuts.

Ingredients
  • Milk 2 Liters, Rice 1/2 cup, Sugar 1 cup, Nuts, Cardamom.
Method
  1. Boil milk and add soaked ground rice.
  2. Cook on low flame till thick, add sugar and garnish with nuts.
Popular

4. Gol Gappay

★★★★★ (4.6)  •  ⏱ 20 min

Crispy hollow puris filled with spicy chickpeas and tangy sour water.

Ingredients
  • Semolina Puri 20, Boiled Chickpeas 1 cup, Tamarind chutney, Mint water.
Method
  1. Poke a hole in puri, fill with chickpeas and potatoes.
  2. Pour cold spicy mint water and sweet chutney inside.
Popular

5. Beef Nihari

★★★★★ (4.9)  •  ⏱ 120 min

Slow-cooked rich beef shank stew garnished with fresh ginger and lemon.

Ingredients
  • Beef shank 1kg, Wheat flour 1/2 cup, Nihari Masala, Oil 1 cup.
Method
  1. Fry meat in oil with masala, cook until tender.
  2. Thicken gravy with wheat flour slurry and simmer.
Classic

6. Chicken Haleem

★★★★★ (4.8)  •  ⏱ 90 min

Wholesome blend of lentils, wheat, and shredded chicken.

Ingredients
  • Mixed lentils 1 cup, Crushed wheat 1 cup, Chicken 500g, Spices.
Method
  1. Boil grains till soft; cook chicken curry separately.
  2. Combine both, blend gently and temper with fried onions.
BBQ

7. Beef Seekh Kebab

★★★★★ (4.7)  •  ⏱ 40 min

Minced meat skewers grilled to perfection with herbs and spices.

Ingredients
  • Minced Beef 500g, Onion 1 (squeezed), Green Chillies, Kebab spices.
Method
  1. Mix mince thoroughly with spices and onion.
  2. Shape on skewers and grill or pan-fry till browned.
Vegetarian

8. Daal Chawal

★★★★★ (4.5)  •  ⏱ 30 min

Comforting yellow lentils served with steamed basmati rice.

Ingredients
  • Yellow Lentils (Moong/Masoor) 1 cup, Rice 2 cups, Garlic, Cumin seeds.
Method
  1. Boil lentils with turmeric and salt till mushy.
  2. Prepare tadka with oil, garlic, and cumin seeds; pour over lentil.
Breakfast

9. Halwa Puri Chana

★★★★★ (4.9)  •  ⏱ 40 min

Traditional Sunday breakfast of fried puris, sweet halwa, and spicy chickpeas.

Ingredients
  • All-purpose flour, Semolina 1 cup, Chickpeas 1 cup, Sugar 1 cup.
Method
  1. Knead dough and deep fry thin puris.
  2. Cook sweet semolina halwa and spicy chickpea gravy.
Special

10. Mutton Paya

★★★★★ (4.7)  •  ⏱ 180 min

Traditional slow-cooked trotters soup rich in flavor and nutrition.

Ingredients
  • Mutton Trotters 4, Onions 2, Whole Spices, Garlic-Ginger paste.
Method
  1. Sauté onions and spices, add trotters and water.
  2. Slow cook overnight or pressure cook for 1 hour till broth thickens.
BBQ

11. Chicken Tikka

★★★★★ (4.8)  •  ⏱ 35 min

Marinated chicken quarter grilled with charcoal smokey flavor.

Ingredients
  • Chicken quarters 2, Red chilli powder, Yogurt 3 tbsp, Lemon juice.
Method
  1. Marinate chicken cuts with lemon and spices.
  2. Grill on coals or bake till charred and cooked.
Creamy

12. Butter Chicken

★★★★★ (4.9)  •  ⏱ 40 min

Tender chicken pieces cooked in a rich, buttery tomato sauce.

Ingredients
  • Boneless Chicken 500g, Butter 50g, Cream 1/2 cup, Tomato puree.
Method
  1. Fry marinated chicken; cook smooth tomato-butter gravy.
  2. Mix chicken into gravy, add fresh cream and fenugreek leaves.
Street Food

13. Samosa Chaat

Crispy potato samosas crushed and topped with chickpeas, yogurt, and chutneys.

Popular

14. Peshawari Chapli Kebab

★★★★★ (4.9)  •  ⏱ 30 min

Famous flat minced beef patties cooked with tomato slices and pomegranate seeds.

Ingredients
  • Minced Beef 500g, Corn flour 2 tbsp, Tomato, Pomegranate seeds, Spices.
Method
  1. Mix mince with coarse spices and corn flour.

15. Gulab Jamun

Soft milk-solid balls fried and soaked in rose-flavored sugar syrup.

  1. Knead smooth dough balls.

Mild

16. Shahi Chicken Korma

Rich gravy cooked with fried crispy onions, yogurt, and kewra water.

  • Chicken 1kg, Yogurt 1 cup, Fried Onions 1 cup, Korma spice.

  1. Whisk yogurt with ground fried onions and spices.

17. Mutton Yakhni Pulao

Basmati rice cooked in seasoned mutton broth for subtle savory flavor.

  1. Blanch and puree spinach.

Healthy

Melt-in-mouth chicken marinated with heavy cream, cheese, and white pepper.

  • Boneless Chicken 500g, Cream 4 tbsp, Green chilli paste, White pepper.
Method

Winter special dessert made with grated carrots cooked slow in milk and ghee.

Classic
