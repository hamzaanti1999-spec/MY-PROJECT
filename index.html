Chicken Biryani — Complete Recipe

A layered chicken biryani with fragrant basmati rice, whole spices, yogurt, tomatoes and aloo bukhara.

Ingredients

  • 2–3 cups aged, long-grain basmati rice, soaked for 30 minutes
  • Whole spices for boiling rice: bay leaves, green cardamom, cloves and cinnamon sticks
  • Salt — a generous amount for parboiling the rice
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee for the boiling water
  • 500 g to 1 kg bone-in chicken pieces
  • ½–¾ cup plain yogurt, well whisked
  • 2 tablespoons fresh ginger-garlic paste
  • 3–4 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 2–3 medium tomatoes, chopped or pureed
  • 3–6 green chillies, slit or chopped
  • 2 green cardamom pods, 2 black cardamom pods, 5–6 cloves, 2–3 cinnamon pieces and 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tsp red chilli powder, 1 tsp black pepper powder and garam masala to taste
  • Biryani masala — a little, to taste
  • Aloo bukhara (dried plums), to taste
  • ¼ cup cooking oil or ghee for frying and cooking the gravy

Cooking Method

  1. In a pan, add oil, turmeric and ginger-garlic paste. Mix and cook briefly.
  2. Add sliced onions and cook until slightly brown.
  3. Add the chicken, mix well, cover and cook on low heat for 5–6 minutes. Remove the lid and check that the water has reduced and the chicken is cooked/tender. Set the chicken aside.
  4. In another pot, heat oil or ghee. Add green chillies, green and black cardamom, whole black pepper, cloves, cinnamon and bay leaves. Fry briefly.
  5. Add red chilli powder, garam masala and black pepper powder. Add the tomatoes and cook.
  6. Add the yogurt and mix well. Cook the mixture, then add the prepared chicken and combine with the masala.
  7. Add aloo bukhara and a little packaged biryani masala.
  8. Boil the soaked basmati rice with the whole spices, salt and 1 tablespoon oil/ghee. Parboil the rice, then drain it.
  9. Layer the rice and chicken gravy: spread a layer of rice, add the chicken gravy over it, then cover with the remaining rice.
  10. Cover and leave on dum for 5–7 minutes. Your biryani is ready to serve.

Serve hot. Garnish as desired and enjoy!

Traditional Beef Nihari — Complete Recipe

Slow-cook this Nihari on low heat until the beef becomes tender and the gravy develops a rich, traditional consistency.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (about 2 lbs) beef shank
  • ½ cup cooking oil or clarified butter (ghee)
  • 2–4 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste

Nihari Spice Blend

  • Fennel seeds and coriander seeds — 1½ tsp each
  • Cumin seeds and black cumin (shahi zeera) — 1 tsp each
  • Whole black pepper and cloves — 1 tsp each
  • Long pepper (pippali) — 2–3 pieces
  • Green and black cardamom, cinnamon and bay leaves
  • Nutmeg (jaiphal) and mace (javitri)
  • Red chilli powder, Kashmiri red chilli powder and turmeric

Thickener & Finishing

  • ¼ cup atta and all-purpose flour mixture, roasted and mixed with cold water into a smooth paste
  • Fresh ginger juliennes, chopped green chillies, fresh coriander, lemon wedges and fried onions

Cooking Method

  1. Dry roast all whole spices in a frying pan on low heat for a short time until fragrant. Let them cool slightly, then grind them into a fine powder.
  2. Mix the ground spices with red chilli powder, Kashmiri red chilli powder and turmeric.
  3. Lightly roast the atta and flour mixture for about 2 minutes. Mix it with the prepared spice blend and about half a glass of cold water to make a smooth paste.
  4. Heat oil or ghee in a large pot. Add the sliced onions and fry until lightly golden.
  5. Add the beef and cook on low heat, stirring occasionally, until it begins to become tender. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a few more minutes.
  6. Add the prepared Nihari spice mixture and mix well with the meat. Add about 2 litres of water, cover the pot and leave it on low heat until the beef becomes very tender.
  7. Gradually add the prepared flour paste while stirring continuously to prevent lumps. Cook until the Nihari becomes rich and thick.
  8. Serve hot and garnish with fresh ginger, green chillies, coriander, lemon wedges and fried onions.
